Sunrise at Tiger Hill does not happen at one fixed time. Between mid-summer and mid-winter the sun rises more than an hour apart at Darjeeling's latitude, and the change happens gradually rather than in a jump. The only reliable answer to "what time is sunrise at Tiger Hill?" is to look up the sunrise time for Darjeeling on your own travel date and then count backwards from it. This page explains how the time moves through the year and how to turn a sunrise time into a departure time.

## Why there is no single sunrise time

The rising time of the sun swings through the year because of the tilt of the Earth's axis. Darjeeling sits far enough north that this swing is large — well over an hour between the earliest and the latest sunrise.

That has a practical consequence. Any page, poster or driver's promise that gives one number for "Tiger Hill sunrise time" is describing a single morning, not the whole year. A time that is right in December can be badly wrong in June.

## The shape of the year

Without quoting a clock time that would only be correct for one week, the pattern runs like this:

- **Earliest sunrises** come around late June, near the summer solstice.
- From late June onwards the sunrise gets **progressively later** into autumn and winter.
- **Latest sunrises** fall around the turn of the year, near the winter solstice.
- From January to June the sunrise gets **progressively earlier** again.

Because the swing is more than an hour, the same "leave at 5" plan does not work all year. Which end of the swing matters most to you also depends on when you travel, since the seasons change the odds of a clear view — see the [best time to visit guide](/best-time-to-visit/).

## How to turn a sunrise time into a departure time

Work backwards in four steps:

1. **Look up the sunrise time for Darjeeling on your date.** A weather app, a sunrise table or a search for "sunrise Darjeeling" plus the date will give you the sunrise clock time. Use your date, not a general figure.
2. **Allow for the light before sunrise.** The sky begins to brighten, and the peaks start to take colour, before the sun reaches the horizon. If you want the whole show, be in place before that.
3. **Subtract the drive.** The viewpoint is up Senchal Road, roughly 11 km from the centre of Darjeeling, and the last stretch is a mountain road that is slow in the dark.
4. **Subtract a buffer.** On busy mornings there can be queueing at the gate and for parking, plus time to walk to a spot. Add a margin rather than planning to the minute.

Then fix the pickup time and the meeting point with your driver the day before. The [early morning departure guide](/early-morning-departure/) covers what is different about leaving before daylight.

## A worked example

Suppose your app says sunrise on your date is at 06:00. If the drive takes about 45 minutes and you want 30 minutes to park, queue and find a place, you would aim to leave your hotel around 04:45 — and to have agreed that time the night before. Change any one number and the departure moves with it, which is exactly why a single "sunrise time" for the whole year is not usable.

## Where the sun rises, and where Kanchenjunga is

One thing that surprises first-time visitors: the sunrise itself does not come up behind Kanchenjunga. The sun rises in the east, while the Kanchenjunga range lies off to the north. The famous "Kanchenjunga sunrise" is really the moment when first light strikes the peaks — the rising sun and the glow on the mountains are two different things to watch. The [Kanchenjunga sunrise guide](/kanchenjunga-sunrise/) explains what to look for.

## Common planning mistakes

- Booking a shared vehicle at a fixed pick-up time that does not match your date's sunrise.
- Assuming the listed 03:00 opening means the sun rises at 03:00.
- Trying to watch the rising sun and the Kanchenjunga glow from the same direction at once.
- Leaving no buffer for the gate queue on a busy morning.

## What this page cannot tell you

This guide does not publish a single fixed sunrise time, because it would be wrong for most of the year. It also cannot promise that the sky will be clear. Sunrise time is predictable; visibility is not. Read the [sunrise weather guide](/sunrise-weather/) before you set expectations, and confirm the sunrise time for your exact date locally.
