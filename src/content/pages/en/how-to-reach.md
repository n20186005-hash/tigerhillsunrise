## The short answer

You reach Tiger Hill by road, and for a sunrise visit that almost always means a private car or a hired taxi you arrange the day before. The drive starts long before any public transport is running, so hoping to flag down a town taxi at 4 a.m. is not a plan. The route climbs west out of Darjeeling town through Ghoom and then follows Senchal Road to the viewpoint — roughly 11 km from the centre of town, with the last stretch on a narrow mountain road.

## Why this is not a normal taxi ride

Tiger Hill is a viewpoint in the Senchal Forest area on the ridge above Darjeeling, not a stop inside town. Everything about the trip is shaped by that one fact:

- It happens in darkness, on a single approach road.
- Everyone is trying to arrive in the same short window before first light.
- The vehicle has to wait for you, or meet you again at an agreed point after sunrise.

Because of those three points, you fix the arrangement in advance rather than improvise on the morning. The [sunrise taxi guide](/sunrise-taxi/) explains exactly what to agree with a driver.

## Option 1 — A private car or hired taxi

This is the standard choice and the one most visitors should use. Hotels and local operators arrange it, and the arrangement matters more than the type of vehicle. What you are really buying is a pickup at a set hour, a driver who knows the road in the dark, a waiting arrangement while you watch the sunrise, and a return into town.

Agree these things before the morning:

- The **pickup time**, worked back from the sunrise time for your date.
- Whether the driver **waits at the viewpoint** or meets you at a set point afterwards.
- Whether the price is for the **whole trip** or per leg.
- Where you will **meet if the crowd separates you**.

## Option 2 — A shared jeep

A shared jeep towards Tiger Hill is sometimes available in season from Darjeeling's jeep stands. Treat availability as unconfirmed: shared services are shaped by demand and are not reliably timed for a pre-dawn departure. If this is your plan, ask locally the day before, and have a taxi as a fallback. A shared jeep also drops you where its route ends, not necessarily at the viewpoint gate.

## Option 3 — Your own vehicle

Self-driving works if you are confident on narrow mountain roads in the dark. Two things decide whether it is sensible: whether you can find the turning for Senchal Road without a passenger navigating, and whether you have a confirmed place to park. Space near the viewpoint is limited on a busy morning. Read the [parking guide](/parking/) before you commit to driving yourself.

## The route via Ghoom

From Darjeeling the road climbs towards Ghoom, the small town on the same ridge. At Ghoom the route turns onto Senchal Road and continues to the viewpoint, passing through Senchal Forest country. In the dark, the useful habits are simple: keep to the main road rather than guessing at side tracks, follow the signs and the line of other vehicles towards Tiger Hill, and slow right down on the last stretch where the road narrows and people are walking.

On the way back, most people are heading the same way, so the descent is easier to plan than the ascent. Many stop at Batasia Loop or Ghoom Monastery, since both are on the same road. See the [Tiger Hill to Batasia Loop route](/tiger-hill-to-batasia-loop/) and the [Ghoom Monastery guide](/ghoom-monastery/) for those add-ons.

## If you are coming from outside Darjeeling

Two arrivals are commonly confused with "getting to Tiger Hill", and both need separating in your head:

- **Bagdogra airport** is a hill journey that brings you to Darjeeling town. See the [from Bagdogra guide](/from-bagdogra/).
- **NJP railway station** is likewise a hill journey to town, not to the viewpoint. See the [from NJP guide](/from-njp/).

Neither delivers you to the sunrise. If you land or arrive the same day you hope to watch the sunrise, the honest answer is that you are cutting it very fine. Plan the arrival day and the sunrise morning as two separate tasks, and read [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) so your hotel is inside town before the pre-dawn start.

## What to confirm the day before

- The sunrise time for your date, and the pickup time that follows from it.
- The meeting point and the waiting arrangement.
- The basis of the price — per vehicle, per trip, or otherwise — without assuming any figure is the official tariff.
- Whether the vehicle can get close to the standing area if anyone in your group walks slowly.

## When to leave

There is no fixed departure hour, because sunrise moves by more than an hour through the year. Work backwards: look up the sunrise time, subtract the drive, subtract a buffer for the queue at the gate, and set your pickup accordingly. The [sunrise time guide](/sunrise-time/) shows the method, and the [early morning departure guide](/early-morning-departure/) turns it into a night-before checklist.

## Limited walking and accessibility

If anyone in your group finds steps or distances hard, ask how close the vehicle can get before you book, rather than after. The [accessibility guide](/accessibility/) lists the questions that matter most.
