## The short answer

If the Tiger Hill sunrise is cloudy, you have two honest choices: **make the drive anyway and hope for a break**, or **change the plan and spend the morning somewhere it does not matter whether the sky clears**. What you should not do is expect a backup to hand you the Kanchenjunga view you came for — no other place will, on a day the range is hidden. This page is about making a cloudy morning worthwhile, not about conjuring a sunrise that is not there.

## First, judge whether the drive is worth it

Cloud at town level is not the same as cloud at the viewpoint. The hill sits higher and the weather can differ between the two. That is why the decision is best made **with your driver**, who can read the local morning conditions and knows how the road and gate look.

Ask two questions:

- Is the top likely to be clear enough to be worth the climb?
- If we go and it is socked in, are we happy to have made the trip anyway?

If the honest answer to the second is no, skip the drive and use the alternatives below. The [sunrise weather guide](/sunrise-weather/) explains how cloud, fog and haze behave here.

## Then, the honest alternatives

None of these replaces the sunrise. Each is worth doing in its own right, which is exactly what you want on a clouded-out morning.

### Ghoom Monastery

Close to the route you would have taken anyway, and a calm early-morning stop. It does not depend on the weather. See the [Ghoom Monastery guide](/ghoom-monastery/).

### Batasia Loop

The spiral railway and war memorial between Ghoom and Darjeeling. Even under cloud, the engineering and the memorial are the point. See the [Tiger Hill to Batasia Loop route](/tiger-hill-to-batasia-loop/).

### Darjeeling town and the Mall

On a cloudy morning, town life carries on. The Mall, Chowrasta, the shops and the monastery at Observatory Hill — which is inside town, distinct from the Tiger Hill viewpoint — fill a morning easily. The [Darjeeling viewpoints guide](/darjeeling-viewpoints/) lists town spots that need no dawn start.

### Tea gardens

A tea estate visit works in almost any weather: the bushes, the process and a tasting do not require a clear sky. See the [tea gardens guide](/tea-gardens/).

### Senchal Lake and forest

If you are already headed towards the hill's forest belt, the lake and sanctuary suit a quiet, walker-friendly morning. Check access locally. See the [Senchal Lake guide](/senchal-lake/) and the [Senchal Wildlife Sanctuary guide](/senchal-wildlife-sanctuary/).

## Should you try again tomorrow?

If your schedule allows, a second sunrise attempt is a fair fallback — the view is weather-dependent, and one cloudy morning says nothing about the next. Weigh two things:

- **Another very early start**, with the same cold and the same wait.
- **Another transport cost**, since the ride is the main expense.

If either is too much, the alternatives above are the better use of the day. The [is it worth it guide](/is-it-worth-it/) looks at this trade-off honestly, and the [monsoon guide](/monsoon-guide/) covers the wettest months, when cloud is the rule rather than the exception.

## How to set expectations

- **A cloudy morning is normal here**, not a sign that you chose the wrong date. Even the clearest months have cloudy days.
- **A forecast is a probability, not a promise.** "Clear" can still mean a hidden range at that exact hour.
- **Do not over-plan around a guaranteed view.** Build the trip so that a hidden sunrise is disappointing but not ruinous.
- **Change the goal.** On a cloudy day, aim for a good morning in the hills rather than a specific photograph.

## A simple cloudy-day plan

1. Check conditions with your driver before committing to the drive.
2. If it looks poor, head to Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop instead.
3. Have breakfast at Ghoom or in town — the [breakfast guide](/breakfast-after-sunrise/) has the options.
4. Fill the rest of the morning with town or tea, whichever suits you.
5. Keep the next morning as a possible second attempt, decided on the day.

Decide in the moment, keep a good-humoured plan B ready, and a cloudy sunrise becomes a different morning rather than a wasted one.
