## The short answer

For the Tiger Hill sunrise, **where you stay matters less than how you leave**. The viewpoint sits on Senchal Road above Darjeeling, reached through Ghoom. Staying on the **Ghoom and Jorebunglow side** shortens the pre-dawn drive; staying in **central Darjeeling** around the Mall gives you more restaurants and shops. Both are workable. What is not negotiable is an early start and a confirmed ride.

This page describes areas and types of accommodation, and the factors that move cost. It does not name specific properties or quote prices, because those change and we have not verified them.

## The main areas, and what each is good for

### Central Darjeeling — the Mall and Chowrasta

The heart of town. You are close to cafés, shops, the Mall and Observatory Hill and Mahakal Temple. It is the most convenient base for a general stay and the widest choice of places to eat and sleep.

The trade-off for the sunrise: you are furthest from the viewpoint, so the dawn drive is a little longer and you leave a little earlier. Remember Observatory Hill here is inside the town — it is not the Tiger Hill sunrise viewpoint, despite the similar name.

### The Ghoom and Jorebunglow side

Ghoom sits on the road you take to Senchal Road, so staying here puts you closer to the sunrise run. This area is quieter and higher, and it suits a trip built around the dawn. It also puts the toy train line and Ghoom Monastery within easy reach. The cost is that town facilities are a short drive away.

### Quieter and greener edges

Darjeeling has calmer areas away from the centre, including places near the forest and along the approach roads. These suit travellers who want quiet and do not mind driving for meals. Check how the property handles a very early departure — access roads can be steep and dark.

## Types of accommodation

- **Hotels** — the widest range, from simple to comfortable. More likely to have a front desk that can arrange a sunrise car.
- **Guesthouses** — smaller, often good value, sometimes with fewer facilities.
- **Homestays** — a room in a family home. Excellent for local advice; confirm transport in advance.
- **Heritage or character properties** — colonial-era and hillside buildings. Charming, but check the walk to your room, which can involve steps.

The [accessibility guide](/accessibility/) is worth reading if anyone in your group finds steps difficult, as many hillside properties are built on slopes.

## What actually drives the cost

We do not publish rates, but the factors that move them are predictable:

- **Season.** The clearer autumn and winter months and any holiday period are busier and costlier.
- **Location.** Central and view-facing rooms usually cost more than back rooms and outlying areas.
- **View rooms.** A room facing the range carries a premium. It is comfort, not a guarantee of a visible sunrise.
- **Breakfast and extras.** Whether breakfast is included and when the kitchen opens matters on a sunrise morning.
- **Transport add-ons.** A hotel-arranged sunrise car may be convenient but is a separate cost.

For a leaner trip, the [budget guide](/budget-trip/) explains where the money tends to go. Always confirm the rate, the cancellation terms and what is included directly with the property.

## Practical tips for a sunrise stay

1. **Ask the front desk** what time the kitchen opens and whether an early breakfast or a packed snack is possible.
2. **Confirm the morning car** the night before, with a meeting point and a fare that is clear about what it covers.
3. **Ask about the door.** Some properties lock the main entrance late; make sure you can leave when you need to.
4. **Check the walk to your room** in daylight, so you are not navigating unknown steps in the dark.
5. **Keep one warm layer unpacked** so you are not repacking at 4 a.m.

## Where to stay is only half the plan

A good base helps, but the sunrise itself depends on timing and weather. Read the [Tiger Hill sunrise time guide](/sunrise-time/) to set your alarm, and the [how to reach guide](/how-to-reach/) for how the drive works. Between the two, you will know whether your accommodation choice is close enough to make the morning easy — and that is the only real test.
