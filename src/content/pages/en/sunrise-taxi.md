## The short answer

You arrange a sunrise taxi the day before, once you know the sunrise time for your date, and the arrangement matters far more than the price. Fix four things: the pickup hour, whether the driver waits or meets you, the basis of the price, and where you will meet if the crowd separates you. We do not quote a fare, because we have not verified a current one — ask locally and treat any figure as changeable.

## Why the arrangement beats the price

On a busy sunrise morning everything converges on one narrow road at one narrow window of time. The problems people actually have are not "the taxi was expensive"; they are:

- The driver arrived before they were ready, or they came down and could not find the car.
- They thought the price included waiting, and it did not.
- They did not agree a meeting point and lost each other in the dark.

Fix those in advance and the money question becomes small.

## What to agree, point by point

**The pickup time.** Work backwards from sunrise: sunrise time, minus the drive up, minus a buffer for the queue at the gate. The [sunrise time guide](/sunrise-time/) shows how. Do not accept a vague "around four"; get an hour, and check it against your date.

**The waiting.** Ask directly: does the driver wait at or near the viewpoint, and until roughly when? Some wait; some park and return at an agreed time. Either can work — the failure is assuming.

**The return.** Is the price for a single drop, or the whole trip including the descent? If it is drop-only, how will you get back?

**The meeting point.** In the dark and the crowd, "the car park" is not precise enough. Name a specific spot — by the gate, by a sign, at the end of a row — and agree it out loud.

**What the price covers.** Ask whether entry, the observatory and parking are included or separate. They frequently are not. See the [entry fee guide](/entry-fee/).

## A short script you can reuse

When you book, say something like: "Pickup at [time] from [hotel]. Do you wait at Tiger Hill while we watch the sunrise, or come back at a set time? Is the price for the whole trip down? Where exactly do we meet? Does it include entry or parking? What is the total?" Write the answers down.

## On the price itself

We deliberately give no number. Fares vary with the vehicle, the distance from your hotel, the season and demand, and we have not verified a current official rate. Two honest rules:

- Treat any quote as a **local, changeable price**, not an official tariff.
- Keep the **transport price separate from any entry or parking** amount, so a later request does not feel like a double charge when it may be a different charge.

Carry small cash, and do not rely on card or UPI at a hill gate before dawn.

## Shared jeeps and the fallback

A shared jeep towards Tiger Hill is sometimes available in season from Darjeeling's jeep stands. It is usually the cheaper per-person option when it runs, but its timing is set by demand and it may not drop you at the gate. Treat it as an option to confirm locally, and keep a taxi as a fallback. The [how to reach guide](/how-to-reach/) compares the choices.

## Where your hotel sits in all this

The distance from your hotel to the pickup point is part of the plan. A hotel inside town near Chowrasta or the main taxi stands makes an early pickup simpler. If you are still choosing, read [where to stay](/where-to-stay/).

## If the morning turns cloudy

You will still pay for the transport you arranged — the trip happened even if the peaks did not appear. Have a fallback ready so the morning is not wasted: the [cloudy day alternatives guide](/cloudy-day-alternatives/) lists what to do instead.

## Walking, seniors and children

If anyone in your group walks slowly, ask the driver how close the vehicle can get to the standing area, and read the [accessibility guide](/accessibility/) and [sunrise with kids guide](/sunrise-with-kids/) before booking.
