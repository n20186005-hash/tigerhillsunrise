## The short answer

We have not verified a current official tariff for visiting Tiger Hill, so we will not quote a number as if it were the price. In practice, a sunrise visit can involve several separate charges — the vehicle and driver, entry to the viewpoint area, possibly the observatory building, and parking — and a driver's single quote may or may not cover all of them. Before you pay anything, ask what each amount is for. Do not assume entry is free, and do not assume the vehicle fare is the entry fee.

## Why we will not give you a figure

Several reasons, and they all point the same way:

- The place sits in the Senchal Forest area and the sunrise access is a practical arrangement, not a fixed public attraction with a single posted gate price we can confirm.
- Charges can be collected by different parties — a driver, a gate, a parking attendant — for different things.
- Third-party listings show a time range but not a verified, current tariff.

A number copied from an old blog or a listing is worse than no number, because you may plan around it and then argue at a dark gate. The honest version is: ask, and treat any figure as local and changeable.

## What may be charged, and by whom

Think of a sunrise visit as a stack, not one fee:

1. **The vehicle and driver.** Usually the largest amount, arranged the day before, and usually quoted for the trip rather than per person. See the [sunrise taxi guide](/sunrise-taxi/).
2. **Entry to the viewpoint area.** May be collected near the gate. Ask who is collecting it and what it covers.
3. **The observatory building.** The word "observatory" in the place name refers to the viewpoint building on Senchal Road, not a promised telescope hall. If access to any enclosed area is charged, ask whether it is included above or separate.
4. **Parking.** Often handled separately, near where vehicles are directed. See the [parking guide](/parking/).

Being asked for four things is not automatically wrong. What matters is knowing which is which.

## The exact questions to ask before paying

- What does this amount **cover** — the vehicle, entry, the observatory, or parking?
- Is it **per person or per vehicle**?
- Is it a **one-off for the trip** or are there further charges on the way down?
- Is there a **ticket or receipt** for a charge described as official?
- If I have already paid the driver, is the **entry still to be paid separately**?

If an answer is vague, that is useful information. Ask again, or note what you were told so you can compare later.

## Opening hours and what a listing means

The Google Maps listing for the viewpoint shows **03:00–17:00, all days**, read on 26 October 2026, with a rating of **4.5 / 5 from 18,431 ratings**. Treat both as a third-party snapshot, not an operator's rule. The hours do not by themselves confirm that vehicles or visitors are admitted at 03:00 on your date, and the rating is a crowd average, not a guarantee of anything.

## Tickets, timings and the sunrise

There is no fixed sunrise hour, so there is no fixed "ticket time". Work back from the sunrise time for your date and agree your arrival with whoever is driving you. The [sunrise time guide](/sunrise-time/) covers the method. If you are weighing whether the whole trip is worth the early start and the cost, read [is it worth it](/is-it-worth-it/).

## Money practicalities

- Carry **small cash**. Do not rely on card or UPI at a hill gate before dawn.
- Keep your **vehicle payment and any entry payment separate in your head**, so a later request does not feel like a double charge when it may be a different charge.
- If you are budgeting a trip, treat the fee as an **unknown to confirm locally**, not a fixed line you can lock in now. The [budget trip guide](/budget-trip/) follows the same approach.

## If the fee does not seem right

If a charge is described as official but no ticket is offered, you are entitled to ask again. You are also entitled to walk away from a service, though not from a place you have already entered. Note the time, the amount and who collected it. This site cannot resolve a dispute, but knowing which charge was which helps you avoid one.
