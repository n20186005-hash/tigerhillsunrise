## The place itself

### What exactly is Tiger Hill?

It is the sunrise viewpoint above Darjeeling, on Senchal Road in the Senchal forest area, roughly 11 km from the town centre. The Google Maps listing calls it "Tiger Hill Observatory / Sunrise Viewpoint". The "observatory" is a place name, not an astronomical observatory with telescopes and exhibits.

### Is Tiger Hill the same as Observatory Hill?

No, and this confuses a lot of visitors. **Observatory Hill** — with the Mahakal Temple — is **inside Darjeeling town** near Chowrasta. **Tiger Hill** is the separate sunrise viewpoint on Senchal Road. If you want the town site, read the [Observatory guide](/observatory-guide/); for the sunrise viewpoint, you are in the right place.

### What is the location and rating?

The listed map point is on Senchal Road, Plus Code X7VP+W3. The Google Maps rating is **4.5 / 5 from 18,431 ratings**, read on 26 October 2026 — that is a third-party snapshot, not a promise about your visit.

## Times and tickets

### What time is the sunrise?

It changes through the year by more than an hour, so there is no single answer. Look it up for your date and work backwards. The [sunrise time guide](/sunrise-time/) explains the method with a worked example.

### What are the opening hours?

The Google Maps listing shows **03:00–17:00, all days**, read on 26 October 2026. Treat this as a **listing only**, not a guarantee that vehicles or visitors are admitted at any given hour on your date.

### How much is entry?

We have **not verified a current official tariff**. Charges for the vehicle, entry, the observatory and parking may be separate or combined. Ask what each amount covers before paying; the [entry fee guide](/entry-fee/) lists the questions to ask.

## Getting there

### How do I reach Tiger Hill?

Most visitors go by **private car or hired taxi**, arranged through a hotel or local operator, because the trip begins before public transport runs. The route runs through Ghoom and up Senchal Road. See the [how to reach guide](/how-to-reach/) and the [sunrise taxi guide](/sunrise-taxi/).

### What about parking?

Parking arrangements near the viewpoint change and are not something we can verify for your date. Confirm with your driver where you will park and where you will meet. The [parking guide](/parking/) covers what to ask.

### Can I get there from Ghoom, NJP or Bagdogra?

Ghoom is the closest approach and the natural stop on the way. NJP and Bagdogra are **hill journeys in their own right** that bring you to Darjeeling town, not a dawn shuttle to the viewpoint. See the [from Ghoom](/from-ghoom/), [from NJP](/from-njp/) and [from Bagdogra](/from-bagdogra/) guides.

## What you will see

### Will Kanchenjunga be visible?

Not always. On a clear morning the Kanchenjunga range catches the first light, and in especially clear conditions a distant Everest appears. Cloud, fog and haze can hide everything. The [Kanchenjunga sunrise guide](/kanchenjunga-sunrise/) and the [Everest view guide](/everest-view/) explain what to look for.

### What if it is cloudy?

Cloud is normal in the hills. Decide with your driver whether to make the drive, and keep a fallback ready. The [cloudy day alternatives guide](/cloudy-day-alternatives/) lists honest options — none of which pretend to replace the mountain view.

## Planning your trip

### What is the best time of year?

There is no month that guarantees a view. October to December and the winter months generally offer clearer skies but colder mornings; the monsoon is the least predictable. See the [best time to visit guide](/best-time-to-visit/), the [winter sunrise guide](/winter-sunrise/) and the [monsoon guide](/monsoon-guide/).

### How much time should I allow?

Allow for the drive each way, any queue, your own walking and how long you want to stand in the cold. A half-day shape is common — see the [half-day itinerary](/half-day-itinerary/), the [two-day itinerary](/darjeeling-2-day-itinerary/) and the [three-day itinerary](/darjeeling-3-day-itinerary/).

### Where should I stay, and what about costs?

The [where to stay guide](/where-to-stay/) explains the areas and accommodation types without endorsing any property, and the [budget guide](/budget-trip/) shows where a sunrise trip's money goes. We do not quote prices we cannot verify.

## Families, safety and caring for yourself

### Is it suitable for children?

Yes with planning. The hard parts are the early start, the cold and the wait. Read the [sunrise with kids guide](/sunrise-with-kids/) and the [family itinerary](/family-itinerary/).

### Is it safe?

It is a low-risk, ordinary outing for most visitors. Manage the cold, the darkness, the mountain road and uneven ground. The [visitor safety guide](/visitor-safety/) covers this, and the [accessibility guide](/accessibility/) helps if anyone finds steps difficult.

### What should I bring?

Layers, a hat, gloves, water, a torch and shoes with grip. Carry your own medication, and consult a professional about any health concern — this guide cannot give medical advice. See the [what to bring guide](/what-to-bring/).
