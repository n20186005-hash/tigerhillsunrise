## What is the Darjeeling toy train, and does it go to Tiger Hill?

The **Darjeeling toy train** is the popular name for the **Darjeeling Himalayan Railway**, a narrow-gauge mountain railway that climbs between the plains and Darjeeling through Ghoom. It is a UNESCO World Heritage line, as documented by the railway tourism source. The important clarification for a sunrise trip: the toy train is a **railway journey, not transport to the Tiger Hill viewpoint**. That viewpoint is on Senchal Road and is reached by road. You can pair a sunrise with a train ride, but the train will not put you on the hill.

## Why the line matters

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway earns its reputation from how it was built: a narrow-gauge line engineered to climb steep mountain terrain, with the Ghoom area at the top of its climb. The railway tourism source records its **UNESCO World Heritage** status, which is the fact worth trusting here. Beyond that, we do not add engineering or historical detail we cannot attribute to an official source.

The [Ghoom railway station guide](/ghoom-railway-station/) covers the specific station, and the [Ghoom monastery guide](/ghoom-monastery/) covers the neighbouring stop, if you plan to linger in Ghoom.

## Where it runs

The line connects the plains up to Darjeeling, passing through Ghoom on the way. In practice, visitors meet it in two ways:

- **Short heritage rides** in the Darjeeling and Ghoom area, which suit people who are already in town.
- **The longer climb** from the plains, which is a day's journey rather than an add-on to a sunrise morning.

We have **not verified** the current origins, destinations, timings or whether a given service stops at Ghoom. Treat any schedule you find as needing confirmation with the operator.

## Fares, timings and booking — what is not verified

We have not verified fares, seat availability, steam-versus-diesel operation, or booking rules. Rather than publish numbers we cannot stand behind, here is what to ask the official booking channel:

- Which services currently operate, and where do they start and end?
- What are the current fares, and are child or senior rates available?
- How far ahead should I book, and is there same-day availability?
- Does my chosen service stop at Ghoom?

Do not assume a seat will be free on the day, and do not treat a reseller's price as the official fare.

## Pairing the toy train with a Tiger Hill sunrise

The two are separate, and that is the point to hold onto. A sensible pairing looks like this:

1. **One morning** — sunrise at Tiger Hill, then the [Ghoom and Batasia stops](/tiger-hill-to-batasia-loop/) on the way down.
2. **A different slot** — a toy train ride, booked separately, at a time that does not clash.

Trying to bolt a train ride onto a pre-dawn sunrise usually means rushing both. The [half-day itinerary](/half-day-itinerary/) and the [two-day itinerary](/darjeeling-2-day-itinerary/) show how to give each its own space. If you are based at Ghoom, start with the [Tiger Hill from Ghoom page](/from-ghoom/).

## Riding respectfully and safely

- **Stay off the tracks** unless you are on a permitted platform or crossing. On a working line, trains can move with little warning.
- **Do not lean out of carriages**, and follow crew instructions.
- **Keep the line clear** for residents and workers who rely on it.
- **Photography** is welcome, but never at the cost of your safety or someone else's.
