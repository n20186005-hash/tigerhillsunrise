## The short answer

There is parking near the Tiger Hill viewpoint, but it is limited and it fills in the pre-sunrise window, when the single approach road is at its busiest. On the morning itself you will usually be directed rather than free to choose a space. The easiest way to deal with this is not to solve it yourself: a hired driver who knows where vehicles are being sent removes most of the problem.

## What a sunrise morning actually looks like

Everyone is converging on one narrow road at one time. In the half-hour or so before first light:

- Vehicles queue along Senchal Road, moving slowly.
- People get out and walk the last stretch.
- Attendants, or the drivers themselves, sort out where vehicles stop.

That is normal, not a sign you did something wrong. The mistake is expecting an empty car park and a leisurely choice of space at 4 a.m.

## If you are being driven

This is the simplest case. Tell whoever is driving that you want to be dropped as close as is sensible and agree where the vehicle will be once you come back. Ask directly whether a parking charge applies and who collects it. Because the driver is in the flow of traffic, they will usually know the routine on the day far better than any blog written months earlier.

Confirm when you book:

- Where you will be **dropped** and where you will be **met**.
- Whether **parking is charged**, and whether it is separate from entry.
- What to do **if the crowd separates** you from the vehicle.

The [sunrise taxi guide](/sunrise-taxi/) covers the wider arrangement.

## If you are driving yourself

Self-driving is workable if you are a confident mountain driver, but parking is the part that decides whether it is sensible. Before you set off:

- Confirm there is somewhere to **leave the vehicle** near the viewpoint rather than assuming.
- Know the **turning for Senchal Road** so you are not guessing in the dark.
- Plan for the **queue** and for walking the last stretch.
- Have a **fallback** if the near area is full.

Do not stop on blind bends, do not block the carriageway, and do not leave a vehicle where it narrows the road for everyone behind you. It is dark, the road is single-lane in places, and people are walking along it.

## What a parking charge means

A parking charge, where it applies, may be collected separately near the viewpoint. We have not verified a current amount, so this page does not quote one. The useful question is not "how much" but "what is this for": is it parking, is it entry, or is it both bundled? Ask, and keep the answer distinct from any entry amount. The [entry fee guide](/entry-fee/) walks through the charges that can be stacked on a single visit.

## Parking and the rest of your morning

If you plan to continue to Batasia Loop or Ghoom Monastery after sunrise, remember those are separate places with their own arrangements. Do not assume the space you used at Tiger Hill covers a stop elsewhere, and do not assume a place is open the moment you arrive at dawn. See the [Tiger Hill to Batasia Loop route](/tiger-hill-to-batasia-loop/) and the [Ghoom Monastery guide](/ghoom-monastery/).

## Safety around the vehicle

- Keep the vehicle **locked** when you leave it.
- Watch your footing on **uneven ground in the dark**; carry a torch.
- Do not leave valuables visible.
- If anyone in your group walks slowly, arrange the drop point accordingly.

The [visitor safety guide](/visitor-safety/) covers the wider do's and don'ts.

## Limited walking and accessibility

If steps or distances are a problem, the drop point matters as much as the parking. Ask how close the vehicle can get to the standing area before you commit. The [accessibility guide](/accessibility/) lists the questions to ask.
